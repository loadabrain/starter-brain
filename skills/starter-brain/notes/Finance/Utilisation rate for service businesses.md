# Utilisation rate for service businesses

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Utilisation tells you how much of your time earns money. It sets the minimum day rate. Benchmarks: SPI Research and Deltek - Billable utilisation benchmark.

## Definition
`utilisation % = billable hours / available hours`. Sources differ on the denominator (fixed 2,000 h vs working time minus approved leave and holidays), so the same person can show different rates: write down your choice.

## Benchmarks (dated)
- 2026 SPI/Deltek benchmark: 66.4 % average, 75 % for high performers.
- 2025 edition: 68.9 %; 70-80 % was historically the profit-maximising range; above 80 % goes with burnout.
- Small teams tend to be lower (second-hand report, unverified).

## Why it matters
- Lower utilisation = fewer billable days = higher day rate needed: see Day rate for a service business or studio.
- High utilisation is not the goal alone: SPI warns low-margin work can fill the calendar. Track project margin next to hours (Checklist - Margin check before quoting).

## Steps
1. Pick the denominator. 2. Log billable vs non-billable (sales, admin, learning) for 8 weeks. 3. Compute monthly %. 4. Set the planning utilisation in [[Template - Pricing calculator]] at your measured value, not at 100 %.

## Update 2026-11-03
SPI data for 2025: billable utilisation 66.4 % (2024: 68.9 %), five-year low; margins and sources in Agency and studio margin benchmarks (dated, with sample).
