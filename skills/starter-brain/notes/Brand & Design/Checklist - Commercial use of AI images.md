# Checklist - Commercial use of AI images

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Free tiers often forbid commercial use or make images public.
> Midjourney needs Pro/Mega above $1M revenue; Recraft free images belong to Recraft.
> Pure AI output may be unprotectable; log tools, models and dates.

## Checklist
- [ ] Which plan was used? Free-tier images: Recraft forbids commercial use; Midjourney has no free tier; Ideogram and Krea unclear.
- [ ] Revenue test: Midjourney requires Pro or Mega for companies over $1,000,000 yearly revenue (Midjourney).
- [ ] Privacy: public by default on Midjourney and free plans; Stealth/private needs Pro/Mega or paid.
- [ ] Output type: Firefly own model vs partner model (Firefly commercial safety and IP indemnity - what is covered).
- [ ] Indemnity: none in Midjourney terms per a legal write-up; Canva: enterprise customers with 100+ seats are indemnified for Magic Studio output, others not (Canva AI (Magic Studio) terms).
- [ ] Copyright: expect no protection for purely generated parts (US Copyright Office AI report Part 2 - copyrightability, Thaler v Perlmutter - human authorship required); add human work and keep drafts.
- [ ] Look-alike: reverse image search, no brand or character names in prompts (Adobe's guidelines forbid prompting for others' copyrighted/trademarked material).
- [ ] Labels: keep Content Credentials (Content Credentials and C2PA for designers); check EU AI Act Article 50 - labelling AI-generated content for realistic synthetic media.
- [ ] Client contract: say whether AI was used and who bears risk.
- [ ] Save terms (PDF + date) and fill Template - AI use log for a client project.

## Law notes
US and EU rules differ; not legal advice. Cite BOIP, EUIPO or WIPO for trade mark questions; ask a lawyer for high-value use.

## Verify pass 2026-11-03
- Midjourney: its plans documentation says a company with more than USD 1,000,000 gross annual revenue must buy Pro or Mega (official page via search extract). Whether ownership depends on the plan is a secondary reading: read the ToS.
- Recraft: current docs say Free-plan images are public, owned by Recraft and not for commercial use; paid-plan output belongs to the user; images made on Free stay Recraft's after an upgrade.
- Adobe: Firefly indemnity depends on plan and model; see Generative AI tools - commercial use and IP indemnity (Adobe Firefly).
