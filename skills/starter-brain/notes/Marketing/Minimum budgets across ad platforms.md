# Minimum budgets across ad platforms

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Snapchat 5 a day, LinkedIn 10 a day, TikTok 20 per ad group and 50 per campaign, Demand Gen 100 a day for Maximize strategies.
> Meta and Pinterest state no hard minimum on the opened pages; Meta suggests a budget that can run at least 7 days.
> Minimums are not recommendations: 50 results in about a week usually needs more than the floor.

## Explanation
Figures as stated on the pages opened on 2026-10-22 (currency as shown on each page, often unnamed). Meta: no minimum on the developer page; consumer pricing page suggests a budget that can run at least 7 days and, in the FAQ, a start of at least 5 over a 6 day run. Google: Performance Max budget panel suggests Low, Medium and High daily budgets with Low as the minimum recommended (amount depends on account); Demand Gen best practice 100 per day for Maximize strategies or 10x target CPA per day; Search: no minimum stated on the pages we opened. TikTok: 50 campaign, 20 ad group (Help FAQ updated August 2026); TikTok for Business recommends 30 daily ad group budget in North America and EMEA. LinkedIn: 10 daily for any format, 100 lifetime for a new campaign (USD). Pinterest: none stated on the budget page; Performance+ guidance is 5x target CPA. Snapchat: 5 per day, recommended 20 to 50. Microsoft: not stated.

The learning-phase arithmetic matters more than the floor: to reach about 50 events in 7 days you need a cost per event of at most 7 times the daily budget divided by 50 (our calculation).

## How to use it
Cost: the floor over a 14 day test, for example Snapchat 70, LinkedIn 140, TikTok ad group 280, Demand Gen 1,400 at 100 per day. Metric: cost per qualified lead at the end of the test. Cheap test: pick the platform where the stated floor for a 7 day test is below what you can afford to lose entirely, and set the test budget in advance as a write-off.

## Trust & evidence
Each figure is first-party from the linked notes (high for the number, with currency often unnamed). Amounts change; the date on this note is the date read.
