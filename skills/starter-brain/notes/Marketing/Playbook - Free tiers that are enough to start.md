# Playbook - Free tiers that are enough to start

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Umami Hobby: 100K events a month, 1 site; PostHog Cloud: 1M events, 5k recordings free; Tally Free: unlimited forms and submissions (fair use).
> Buffer Free: 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts per channel; Metricool Free: 1 brand, 20 posts a month; Figma Starter: unlimited drafts, 500 AI credits a month.
> Framer Free: Framer domain, 1 GB bandwidth; n8n and listmonk are free to self-host but need a server; Later has no free plan.

## Explanation
Limits as stated by vendors on 2026-10-22. Analytics: Umami Analytics Hobby (100K events, 1 website, 6 months retention) or PostHog cloud free tier (1M events, 5k recordings, 1M flag requests, 100k exceptions, 1,500 survey responses; vendor figures). Forms and surveys: Tally Free (unlimited forms and submissions within fair use), Formbricks cloud free plan (limits not verified). Scheduling: Buffer Free (3 channels, 10 queued posts per channel, 30 days of insights), Metricool Free (1 brand, 20 scheduled posts a month, 5 competitors). Design and video: Figma Starter (unlimited drafts, 150 AI credits a day up to 500 a month), CapCut Free. Site: Framer Free (Framer domain, 1 GB bandwidth). Experimentation: GrowthBook free cloud account (limits not verified). Training: Meta Blueprint, Google Skillshop. Debugging: Meta Ads Data Advisor. Not free: Later (trial only), Foreplay (trial with card).

Verdict (judgement): for a solo operator with fewer than a few thousand visits a month these cover analytics, forms, scheduling and design. The limits you will hit first are the 10 queued posts on Buffer Free, the Framer domain, and Umami's single site.

## How to use it
Cost: 0. Metric: which limit is hit first. Cheap test: stay on free tiers for 30 days; upgrade only the tool whose limit blocks revenue work (see Playbook - Tool stack by budget). Always read the fair-use wording and the data location before collecting personal data.

## Trust & evidence
Limits are vendor-stated and can change (medium). The verdict is judgement.
